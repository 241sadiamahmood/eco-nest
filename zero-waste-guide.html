ECO LIFE GUIDE

Your Journey to Zero Waste

Small everyday choices can create a big difference. Learn simple and practical ways to reduce waste, reuse resources and live a more sustainable lifestyle.

Start Your Journey 7-Day Challenge
THE ZERO WASTE MINDSET

Follow the Right Order

Zero waste starts before something becomes waste. Think about what you buy, how long you can use it and what happens when you no longer need it.

🚫

Refuse

Say no to unnecessary products, packaging and disposable items.

📉

Reduce

Buy only what you need and avoid unnecessary consumption.

🔄

Reuse

Choose durable products that can be used again and again.

🔧

Repair

Fix broken products before replacing them with something new.

♻️

Recycle

Recycle suitable materials through your local recycling system.

STEP 01

Start Small. Make It Last.

You don't need to completely change your lifestyle overnight. Begin with small habits and gradually build a sustainable routine.

1

Carry a reusable water bottle and shopping bag.

2

Choose products with less unnecessary packaging.

3

Repair, donate or repurpose products before throwing them away.

4

Separate recyclable and organic waste where practical.

PRACTICAL GUIDANCE

Make Every Room Greener

Discover simple changes you can make throughout your everyday life.

🍎 Kitchen

Reduce food and packaging waste with smarter kitchen habits.

✓ Plan meals before shopping.

✓ Store food correctly.

✓ Use reusable containers.

✓ Compost suitable food scraps.

✓ Choose refill options when available.

🧼 Bathroom

Replace unnecessary disposable personal-care products.

✓ Choose refillable products.

✓ Use washable towels.

✓ Avoid unnecessary packaging.

✓ Choose durable grooming tools.

🛍️ Shopping

Make thoughtful purchasing decisions before products enter your home.

✓ Carry reusable bags.

✓ Buy durable products.

✓ Avoid impulse purchases.

✓ Choose minimal packaging.

WASTE SORTING

Know Where Your Waste Goes

Always check your local recycling guidelines because accepted materials can vary.

📄

Paper

Clean cardboard and suitable paper products may be recyclable.

🥫

Metal

Some cans and metal containers can be recycled through local programs.

🍾

Glass

Glass bottles and jars may be accepted by local recycling programs.

🍎

Organic

Suitable food scraps can be composted where facilities are available.

🌱 Turn Scraps Into Soil
STEP 04

Start Composting

Composting can transform suitable organic waste into a useful soil amendment.

🥬 Greens

  • Fruit scraps
  • Vegetable scraps
  • Coffee grounds
  • Grass clippings

🍂 Browns

  • Dry leaves
  • Twigs
  • Shredded paper
  • Wood chips
